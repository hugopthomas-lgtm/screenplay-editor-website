02What it will not do

It formats your script.
It does not write it.

The tool takes care of margins, indents, scene headings and character cues. The words stay yours, and we never train anything on them.

Two features do use artificial intelligence, a table read with synthetic voices and a production breakdown, and you decide when to run them. Nothing gets written in your place.
