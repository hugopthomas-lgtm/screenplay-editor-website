Ask your question
Add-on or extension, which one should I pick?
The add-on lives in the Google Docs sidebar and works in any browser: it is the safe, universal choice. The extension runs in Chrome and adds the live writing workflow (Enter, Tab, instant formatting as you type): it is the one I use to write. You can try both for free, and one subscription covers both, so you never have to choose for money reasons.
What happens if I cancel?
You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for, then you go back to the free plan. Your script stays exactly as it is: it lives in your Google Drive, not with us, and the formatting already applied never disappears.
Do you see or store my script?
No. Your script stays in your own Google Drive, behind the same security as Gmail. We never store it, we never train anything on it.
Do I pay twice if I use both the extension and the add-on?
No. There is one price and one subscription, and it unlocks both tools on every computer you write on. The $7.99 is a launch price, and people who subscribe now keep it.
