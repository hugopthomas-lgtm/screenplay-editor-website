INT. STAFF ROOM - DAY

Thirty exam scripts sit in a pile on the table, still in their plastic wallets. MARGARET (58), head of film, pours a coffee she will not drink and looks at the pile the way you look at weather.

MARGARET
Who taught them to put the dialogue in the middle of the page?

DEV (24), teaching assistant, first term, stands in the doorway with his coat still on. He looks at his shoes, then at the pile, then back at his shoes.

DEV
I did. I mean, the software did.

MARGARET
Then the software marks them.

She pushes the pile across the table towards him. It stops exactly where he can reach it, which is worse than if it had fallen.
