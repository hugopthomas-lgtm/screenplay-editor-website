THE FIRST SCREENPLAY WRITTEN BY TWENTY PEOPLE AT ONCE

Room 306, Oxford Brookes, 12 September 2026.

One film. Twenty scenes. Take a number nobody has taken, put your first name next to it, and write that scene. Do not coordinate. Do not read the others first.

It is night. Somewhere in a building, a light is on.
