Getting started

After installing from the Marketplace or Chrome Web Store, open any Google Doc, go to Extensions > Screenplay Editor, and click "AI Live Formatting". Just write. Your screenplay formats itself.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I format a scene heading?

Just type. With AI Live Formatting enabled, your screenplay formats automatically as you write. No buttons needed.

Can I collaborate with others?

Yes. You get all Google Docs collaboration features: real-time editing, comments, suggestion mode, and sharing.

How do I export my script?

Use the Export button in the sidebar. Choose PDF or FDX format. The file saves directly to your Google Drive.

Is my script private?

Absolutely. Your content never leaves Google's servers. We don't see your work, ever.

Does it work offline?

Screenplay Editor requires an internet connection. Google Docs' offline mode may allow limited functionality.

How much does it cost?

Screenplay Editor is $7.99/month, or $69/year (launch price, normally $9.99), and one subscription covers both the Chrome extension and the Google Docs add-on. Start free with all core formatting features. Pro unlocks Final Draft export, scene numbers, production breakdown, ElevenLabs Table Read, and removes the PDF watermark.

What is AI Live Formatting?

AI Live Formatting automatically detects and formats your screenplay elements as you type. Scene headings, action, dialogue, parentheticals. Type = before character names and we handle the rest.

What is the Boneyard?

A place to store your cut ideas. Scenes, lines, notes you're not ready to delete. Everything stays attached to your document.

What is Scene Board?

A visual representation of your story structure. See all your scenes on cards, drag and drop to reorganize. Your script updates itself.

How do I manage my subscription?

Open Settings in the sidebar and click "Manage subscription". You can change your plan or cancel anytime through the Stripe Customer Portal.

What happens if I cancel Pro?

You keep access until the end of your billing period, then your account returns to the free plan. Your scripts are never affected. They stay in your Google Drive.

Is there a Chrome extension?

Yes. Screenplay Editor is available as both a Google Docs add-on and a Chrome extension. Both connect to the same features and your same account.
