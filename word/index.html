What it does.

Everything lives in a side panel next to your pages. Word keeps the document, we keep the format.

Write

Enter and Tab that know the element

A template loaded at start makes Enter and Tab instant and contextual, the way Final Draft does it. Capitals for headings, characters and transitions come by themselves.

Format

Format my document, in one click

Paste a script from anywhere, or import a Final Draft, Fade In, Celtx or Fountain file. Every line gets its element and its style.

Page

Title page, scene numbers, page numbers

A title page set the way readers expect, scene numbers you can add and remove, page numbers with the period, Letter or A4.

Studio

Script Stats and Scene Board

Pages, scenes, cast, locations, day and night, the longest scene, in the panel and as a PDF. A board of your scenes in the browser, and the document follows the order you give it.

Studio

Table Read and Screenplay Poster

A different voice for each character, the narrator for the action, your whole script read out loud in the panel. And a movie poster drawn from your script, the one a festival would print.

Ship

PDF, Final Draft, Fountain, Print View

Word renders the PDF with your styles. The .fdx opens in Final Draft as is. The .fountain is plain text you can keep forever. Print View turns the pages like a bound script, in your browser.

Breakdown

Production breakdown

Cast, props, wardrobe, vehicles, effects, scene by scene, in the panel and in a coloured spreadsheet.

Questions.

Does it change my document?

It adds six paragraph styles and uses them. Turn it off, remove it, the document stays a normal Word document in Courier 12 with its styles.

Does it work offline?

Enter, Tab, capitals and the styles come from the template and work without a connection. The pane needs one.

Word for the web, Word on iPad?

Not yet. The template that makes writing instant only exists on the desktop.
