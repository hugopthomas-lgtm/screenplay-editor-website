Free tools for screenwriters.

These run in your browser. Your script stays on your computer, nothing is uploaded, and none of them asks you to create an account.

Converter

Open your screenplay in Google Docs

Drop in a PDF, a Final Draft file or a Fountain file, and get back a document that opens in Google Docs already formatted. It reads the columns of a PDF to work out what is dialogue and who is speaking.
Coming next

Check your format before you send it

Drop in a PDF and see what a reader would flag: margins, dialogue width, scene headings, page count against running time. I am building this one next.
