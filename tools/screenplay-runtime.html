Why one page is not one minute

Across thousands of finished films, the average script runs about 1.1 pages for every minute on screen, so a page is closer to 55 seconds than to 60. The rule is roughly right for a balanced script and drifts by 15 to 20 percent as soon as a script leans hard towards dialogue or hard towards action.

That is why this gives you a range rather than a number, and why it shows you the split. A script that is three quarters dialogue is not wrong, but it will not time like the rule says, and it is better to know that before someone else tells you.

Ask me something
How accurate is a page count as a runtime?
For a script with a normal balance of dialogue and action it lands within about five percent. Outside that it drifts by fifteen to twenty percent, which is why this tool gives a range and shows you which way your script leans.
How long is a 90 page screenplay?
About 82 minutes, taking a page as 55 seconds rather than 60. If the script is dialogue heavy it will play longer than that, and if it is action heavy it will usually play shorter.
Does it count the title page?
No. A title page is not screen time, so it is left out of the count.
Is my script uploaded?
No. The reading happens in this page on your own computer, and nothing is stored anywhere.
